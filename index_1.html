📖 Theory

What is cheminformatics?

Chemistry + computer science + data analysis, used to store, process, visualise and analyse chemical information.

SMILES

Simplified Molecular Input Line Entry System: a text string describing a structure. Ethanol CCO, benzene c1ccccc1, acetic acid CC(=O)O. Parentheses mark branches; = double, # triple; lowercase = aromatic.

Descriptors

Molecular weight, LogP (lipophilicity), TPSA (topological polar surface area), H-bond donors/acceptors, rotatable bonds, ring count, Fsp3.

Lipinski’s Rule of 5

Oral drugs tend to have MW ≤ 500, LogP ≤ 5, H-bond donors ≤ 5, acceptors ≤ 10. One violation is usually tolerated.

3D models

Ball & stick atoms as spheres, bonds as sticks · Stick emphasises bonding · Space filling approximate volume · Wireframe simplified lines.
